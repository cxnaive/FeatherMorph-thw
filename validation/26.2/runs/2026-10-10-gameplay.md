## Bugs Fixed

- Sonic damage was inside the particle loop and pushed the same target repeatedly.
  The test measured Z velocity 22.9; after guarding the first hit, it measures 2.9
  (2.5 sonic impulse plus vanilla damage knockback), with exactly 10 damage.
- Ghast defaults passed 16 into the target-distance overload instead of execute
  delay: shots required a target and skipped charging. Defaults now use no target
  requirement and 16-tick charging. Skill package version 13 migrates only the
  recognized old generated projectile option; cooldown, abilities and customized
  projectile settings are preserved. Two startup fixtures verify migration and
  custom preservation with the complete resulting JSON.
- The noncustom chat branch called the original renderer and discarded its return.
  It now installs a wrapper that supplies the disguise display name and delegates
  source, message and viewer to the original renderer. Both custom and original
  renderers, and disabled override, pass explicit display-name assertions.

Earlier failed runs remain on disk and are not counted as passes. They also exposed
fixture issues: no available disguise, unshaded test dependency descriptors, destroyed
terrain, creative flight expectations, a damage source without direct entity,
asynchronous config propagation and a cooldown-reset ordering race.
